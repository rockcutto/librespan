LibreSpan 0.9.0 — Notices and attribution
================================================

LibreSpan is a modern Android XMPP messenger.

Application license and lineage
-------------------------------

The LibreSpan Android application is distributed under the GNU General Public
License version 3. The complete GPLv3 text is in LICENSE and is also bundled as:

  src/main/assets/licenses/librespan/GPL-3.0.txt

Source lineage:

  Conversations -> Narayana fork line / AnotherIM -> NeoCont (working repository name) -> LibreSpan

Conversations is the principal upstream ancestor. Its original developer and
principal upstream maintainer is Daniel Gultsch (iNPUTmice):
  https://codeberg.org/iNPUTmice

The direct baseline used by this repository came through the Narayana fork line,
including Conversations Classic / AnotherIM. Historical Narayana identifiers are
therefore retained as provenance where they still occur in source or compatibility
metadata.

Upstream per-file copyright and license notices are retained in the source tree
and remain authoritative for those files.

  Marek Palatinus

LibreSpan recovery dictionary ID:

  librespan-ru-pairs-v1

The curated Russian adjective and noun protocol dictionaries are distributed
under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC-BY-SA-4.0).

Frequency basis:
  Digital Pushkin Lab — Russian-Word-Frequency-Lists-for-Children
  file: wordlist_detcorpus_50000.csv
  license: CC0-1.0
  https://github.com/Digital-Pushkin-Lab/Russian-Word-Frequency-Lists-for-Children

Lexical/POS/proper-name validation source:
  OpenRussian / Badestrand russian-dictionary
  license: CC-BY-SA-4.0
  https://github.com/Badestrand/russian-dictionary

The resulting ordered LibreSpan dictionary files contain selected Russian tokens,
not OpenRussian translations, examples or inflection tables. The immutable protocol

  src/main/kotlin/eu/siacs/conversations/security/recovery/
