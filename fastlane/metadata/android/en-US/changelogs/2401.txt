LibreSpan 0.9.1 is a security-hardening maintenance release.

- Hardened App Lock and High Security recovery authentication with Android Keystore-backed cryptographic proof.
- Restricted temporary image and video cleanup to controlled application directories.
- Hardened UnifiedPush registration sender verification.
- Hardened imported content URI handling and image processing.
- Fixed mutable implicit PendingIntent usage.

No feature scope expansion in this release.
