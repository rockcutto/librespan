LibreSpan is a modern Android XMPP messenger derived from Conversations through AnotherIM.

It keeps the open, provider-independent XMPP model: sign in with your existing JID and use the services supported by your XMPP server.

Highlights:
• OMEMO end-to-end encrypted messaging
• Audio and video calls over Jingle/WebRTC
• Images, files, voice messages, audio and video
• Group chats and channels (MUC)
• Message Archive Management (MAM)
• HTTP Upload and UnifiedPush support
• Secure Content Store for protected local message and media data
• App Lock and optional High Security protection for supported devices
• Local search and a simplified modern Android interface

LibreSpan does not require a project-specific XMPP provider. Server capabilities are discovered through XMPP.

The project is free and open-source software. LibreSpan is based on the Conversations family and preserves upstream attribution and licensing.
